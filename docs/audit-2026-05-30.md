## Stable Boundaries

- `Save` remains the only real boundary for filename sync and working TIFF writes.
- Internal working data remains `.storyboard-workbench/layers/*.png` plus `storyboard.project.json`.
- Photoshop layered TIFF is a Save/Export/Import interchange path, not the internal working format.
- Renderer drawing, erasing, selection transform, layer thumbnails, and undo/redo should continue to target bitmap layer PNG assets.
- Legacy `sketchLayers` and `imageLayerEdits` are migration inputs only.

## Hotspots

- `src/renderer/App.tsx` is the main collaboration hotspot. It owns app state, menus, keyboard shortcuts, layer panel behavior, shot cards, and `SketchPad`.
- `electron/main/main.ts` owns IPC, Save orchestration, import, scanning, TIFF metadata, bitmap asset creation, recent folders, and shortcut settings.
- `scripts/electron-bitmap-smoke.mjs` is the main end-to-end safety net, but it is long and should be kept readable as scenarios accumulate.
- `src/renderer/styles.css` has base styles followed by later dark-theme overrides; future visual work should avoid ad hoc tail overrides.

## P0 Guardrails

- Do not rewrite Save orchestration as part of cleanup. It currently preserves rename, import, TIFF replacement, metadata write, commit, and rollback ordering.
- Do not move TIFF baking into autosave or drawing paths.
- Do not replace Photoshop layered TIFF Save with the internal PNG layer format or vice versa.
- Do not split `App.tsx` state management until smaller component extractions are complete and smoke coverage is still green.
